    Choose a photo from your phone or computer. Fabric, tiles, a painted wall or a flower all work, and photos taken in daylight match best. On a computer you can also drag a photo into this box.

    Tap or click any spot on the photo to pick its colour. With a keyboard, move to the photo and use the arrow keys.

    Your photo stays on this device. It is read here in your browser and is never uploaded to Amco or to anyone else.

    What you will get

    • The three closest Amco shades to any spot you pick on the photo.
    • The main colours in the whole photo, each beside its nearest Amco shade.
    • One tap to save a match to your project or open it in the shade card.

    In each pair, the left half is the colour from your photo and the right half is the Amco shade.
