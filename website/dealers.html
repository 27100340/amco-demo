Check a tin is genuine

Do this before the painter opens the tin, while the shop will still take it back.

اصلی ڈبہ پہچانیں

Counterfeit paint and refilled tins are a known problem in Pakistani paint markets. They look right on the shelf, then fade, peel or dry patchy within months, and you pay for the whole job twice.

Every Amco tin carries a batch code on the sticker on its lid. It is the letters AMC followed by two groups of four digits, like this: AMC-0000-0000. Type it in and we check it against our packing records.

Demo codes for this prototype. Try AMC-2610-4827, AMC-2609-1175 or AMC-2611-3390. Any other code in the right format shows the warning.

It looks like AMC-0000-0000.
