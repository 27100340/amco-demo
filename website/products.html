Good, better, best: where to spend and where to save

You do not need our most expensive paint on every wall. A sensible house uses two or three grades. This is how we would split the interior.

اچھا، بہتر، بہترین

Smart Super Emulsion

Economy grade

Save here. Use it on ceilings and store rooms. Nobody touches a ceiling, so washable paint up there is money wasted.

Keep it out of kitchens, bathrooms and anywhere hands reach. It marks easily and it cannot be washed.

Cover Up Special Emulsion

Good grade

The sensible default. Right for a whole-house repaint, guest rooms and rented portions. It wipes with a damp cloth.

It will not survive hard scrubbing. If the budget is tight, this on the walls and Smart Super Emulsion on the ceilings makes a decent house.

Cover Up Plastic Emulsion

Better grade

Worth it where walls get rubbed. Kitchens, corridors and stairs take a scrub every week. The soft sheen also lifts small, dark rooms.

Sheen shows uneven plaster more than matt does, so get the putty right first.

Cover Up Aqueous Matt

Best grade

Spend here. Use it where children touch the walls and where guests sit: the drawing room, the lounge and the children's bedrooms. Tea, crayon and fingerprints wipe off.

It is wasted on ceilings and store rooms.

Three places not to save

  • Prime every wall first. One coat of Smart Series Wall Primer costs far less than repainting after salt (shora) burns through a new topcoat.
  • Use the paint at the strength on the tin. Thinning it to stretch a drum gives you three watery coats that wear out before two proper ones.
  • Keep economy emulsion out of kitchens and bathrooms. Steam and splashes ruin it within months, and it cannot be washed.
