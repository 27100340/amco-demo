Placeholder stock photography for the landing page and page headers, until Amco
supplies its own shoot. Every photo is used under the Unsplash License
(https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission needed,
attribution appreciated but not required. None of them is an Unsplash+ image.

Each slot is saved as webp at 640, 1280 and 1920 px wide (`<slot>-<width>.webp`).
To replace a photo, keep the slot name and update its alt text, focal point and,
for hero photos, its wall mask in `assets/app/content/photos.js`.
