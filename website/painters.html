The Amco Ustad Club

For painters and contractors who use Amco on their jobs. Every Amco tin you buy earns points, and your points set your tier and your rewards.

امکو استاد کلب

  • Points on every Amco tin you buy, recorded from the batch code on the tin or from your dealer bill.
  • Points count per calendar year, January to December. Your total sets your tier for the next year.
  • A quiet year drops you one tier at most, never more.
  • There is no login. Send us your Ustad code on WhatsApp and we tell you your points.

Reward values are indicative and will be confirmed by Amco.

Questions painters ask

عام سوالات

How do I check my points?

On WhatsApp. Send us your Ustad code and we reply with your points this year and your tier.

When does my tier change?

Once a year, in January. Your points from January to December set your tier for the next year. You can go up any number of tiers, but you drop one tier at most.

Does Amco take a cut of my rate?

No. You agree the rate with the customer yourself. Amco only passes the booking on to you.
