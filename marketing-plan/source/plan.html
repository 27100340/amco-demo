Section 1

The plan on one page

SurmaAM-7008

Amco has made paint in Lahore since 1985 and sells it as Cover Up Paints. Online the brand is hard to find and harder to trust: a website built in 2010, three names in use, and a web address on every advert that now leads to a paint company in India. Its larger rivals are online, yet almost none help a customer do the three things that decide a paint purchase: see the colour, know the cost and trust the tin.

The idea

Amco cannot outspend Brighto's celebrity films or Gobi's cricket sponsorships. It can be more useful than all of them. We position Amco as the paint brand that shows you everything before you buy, under one line: Pehle dekho, phir rango (see first, then paint).

Three proofs carry that line, and all three are built into the new website:

  • See the colour. A 3D house you can walk through and repaint, in daylight, bulb light and tube light. No Pakistani paint brand offers this in a browser.
  • Know the cost. Prices published per litre and per tin, and a calculator that answers in rupees. Competitor calculators stop at litres.
  • Trust the tin. A batch-code check against counterfeit and refilled tins.

The colour range itself becomes the brand story: Rang-e-Pakistan, 63 shades named after places, food and seasons every Pakistani knows.

What we will do

  • Fix the foundations first: one name, one web address and one phone number on every tin and advert.
  • Launch the website as the hub, with WhatsApp as the single conversion point feeding dealers.
  • Publish five to six posts a week on Instagram and Facebook, two a week on LinkedIn, and reuse every video on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
  • Build pages around what Pakistanis actually search (prices, shade cards, colour combinations, seepage fixes) and make them quotable by AI assistants.
  • Run five campaigns timed to the 2027 season: launch, price transparency, the pre-Eid peak in Ramadan, Pakistan Day and summer.
  • Start the Ustad Club for painters, who steer a large share of brand choice.

How paint is actually bought

Four people shape the choice: the homeowner, the painter, the dealer and the architect, plus word of mouth.4 The homeowner usually chooses the colour. The painter and dealer often choose the brand. One industry executive put the repaint cycle at every two years in Punjab and every year in Sindh.4

The token problem

Many brands hide redeemable tokens in the tin, worth roughly Rs 50 to Rs 500, which the painter keeps.9 The Competition Commission fined Diamond Paints Rs 5 million in August 2024 for not disclosing this in advertising.9 Master Paints has advertised with the line "Paint kay paisay do, token kay nahi". The homeowner's suspicion is real and current.

The lead problem

Pakistan's standard limits lead in paint to 90 ppm. A study published in December 2025 found 59 of 84 brands tested were over the limit, almost all of them small makers.8 A small brand that can show a clean certificate stands apart from its own tier. Amco's status must be verified before we say anything.

What customers complain about

The shade not matching the card, fading, peeling, damp and fungus, and paint covering less than the tin promised.12 Each is answered by a tool on the new website.

The 2027 season is compressed

Ramadan from about 8 February

The pre-Eid repaint window, mid-January to early March, falls inside the wedding season.19

Two Eids ten weeks apart

Eid ul-Fitr about 9 or 10 March, Eid ul-Adha about 16 or 17 May, subject to moon sighting.19

Monsoon, July to September

Exterior work stops and damp starts. This is trade convention until Amco shares monthly sales.2

Seven things nobody offers

A walk-through house, lighting preview, a rupee calculator, climate advice as a tool, wall diagnosis, a tin check and Urdu. All seven are in Amco's new site.

Calculators stop at litres

Every competitor calculator we could read answers in litres for one coat. None says how many drums to buy or what it costs, which is the question people actually have.

The basics are broken

Dead app links, a shop priced in dollars, a dealer map that does not load, a "book now" button that goes nowhere. Trust is lost on details like these.

TikTok and YouTube Shorts: an addition we recommend

  • YouTube Shorts: the same cuts. YouTube also feeds Google results and AI answers.

Only Brighto has built a real YouTube audience (about 530,000). Master, Berger, Diamond, Gobi's and Nippon each sit under 6,000.15

  • Lead sheet: date, source, city, job size, dealer, outcome. This is the key report.

Painters steer brand choice, and the industry's usual tool, hidden tokens, has drawn a regulator's fine and a rival's attack advert.9 The Ustad Club is the open alternative: rewards the customer can see.

Section 12

GEO: being the answer in AI search

JamunAM-4009

Generative engine optimisation means being named when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers "which paint should I buy in Lahore". It is early in Pakistan, which is the reason to move now.

15%of Pakistanis have used AI tools; 26% of under-30s7
59%of AI chatbot use in Pakistan is ChatGPT; Gemini 32%6

Google's AI answers work in Urdu, and among online shoppers in one 2026 study, 56% used AI to compare prices.18 One competitor, Diamond, already has "Ask ChatGPT" buttons on its site.

What the evidence says works

  • Google states there are no special technical requirements: a page must be indexed and useful.14
  • In a study of 75,000 brands, appearing in AI answers tracked most closely with how often the brand is mentioned across the web, far more than with links.14
  • Research on generative search found that adding statistics, quotations and cited sources raised visibility by up to 40%.14

GEO is young and these findings are correlations. We treat it as a low-cost extension of good SEO, measured monthly, not as a guaranteed channel.

Eight actions for Amco

  1. Answer first. Every page opens with a two-sentence answer containing a number and a date.
  2. Publish quotable facts. Price per litre, coverage per coat, drying time, cost per square foot, painted cost of a 5 marla house.
  3. Use real tables and structured data so machines can read prices and products.
  4. One identity everywhere. Same name, address and phone on the site, Google, LinkedIn, PCA, LCCI and Wikidata.
  5. Be mentioned where assistants read: YouTube (with transcripts), property portals, price blogs, Reddit and Quora answers given honestly, trade press.
  6. Write question pages in Urdu and Roman Urdu. Almost no brand content exists in either.
  7. Let AI crawlers in. Allow them in robots.txt and add a plain-text summary file of key facts.
  8. Add "Ask AI about this" links on product pages that open an assistant with a prepared question.

How we will measure it

A fixed panel of 20 questions, asked monthly in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers, in English and Roman Urdu. We record whether Amco is named, cited or absent, and who is named instead.
